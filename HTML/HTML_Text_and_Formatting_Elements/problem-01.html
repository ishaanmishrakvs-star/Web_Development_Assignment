About The College

GLA University, located in Mathura, Uttar Pradesh, is a reputed private university established in 2010. It offers undergraduate, postgraduate, and doctoral programs in engineering, management, pharmacy, law, science, humanities, and other fields. The university has a modern campus with well-equipped laboratories, libraries, hostels, sports facilities, and various student clubs. GLA focuses on academic excellence, innovation, research, skill development, and industry exposure. It also provides internship and placement opportunities through its dedicated training and placement cell. With its modern infrastructure and diverse academic programs, GLA University provides students with a supportive environment for learning and overall development.

Campus Life

GLA University offers a vibrant campus life with various extracurricular activities, clubs, and events. Students can participate in sports, cultural festivals, technical competitions, and social initiatives. The university has well-equipped hostels, recreational facilities, and a supportive environment that encourages personal growth and skill development. Students can engage in workshops, seminars, and guest lectures to enhance their knowledge and network with industry professionals. The campus also promotes a sense of community and collaboration among students from diverse backgrounds.

Why I Like My College
